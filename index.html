Biography

I am currently a Researcher in the BlueImage Lab at vivo Mobile Communication Co., Ltd (with Dr. Bo Li). Prior to my current role, I was a research intern at Tencent Youtu Lab, the BlueImage Lab at vivo, Tencent AMS and Midea AI Lab. I received the B.S. degree from Xidian University in Jun 2020, and the Ph.D. degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in Jun 2025, advised by Prof. Jia Wang.
